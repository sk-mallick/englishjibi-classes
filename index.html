Admissions Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining ENGLISHJIBI Classes.

Can my child attend a free trial or demo class before enrolling?

Yes! We offer a free 1-day demo session so parents and students can experience Chiranjibi Sir's teaching methodology firsthand before finalizing admission.

What is the maximum batch size at ENGLISHJIBI Classes?

We maintain strict batch caps of 15 to 20 students per class. This guarantees that every student receives individual guidance, personal feedback, and opportunities during oral drills.

Which school boards do you cover?

Our curriculum is tailored specifically for CBSE & NCERT pattern students from top Bhubaneswar schools like DAV, ODM, DPS, SAI International, Narayana, KIIT, and Mother's Public School.

How are parents updated on student academic progress?

We conduct regular weekly written tests and oral evaluations. Test results and monthly performance cards are shared directly with parents via WhatsApp and regular PTM sessions.

Where is the coaching center located in Bhubaneswar?

Our institute is conveniently located at Duplex 37, Sailashree Vihar, Bhubaneswar, Odisha – 751021. Easy access for students from Patia, Sailashree Vihar, Damana, and Chandrasekharpur.
